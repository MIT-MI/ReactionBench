What to do

  1. The clip plays from the start once, so you see how this reviewer normally behaves.
  2. The bar under the video marks the moments to score (blue = current). You only score these marked moments — the grey gaps between them are not scored.
  3. For each marked moment, press 0–3. Scoring one jumps to the next. Click any marker to re-watch that exact moment.

The 0–3 scale

0
calm: no visible or audible reaction.
1
mild: slight smile, nod, small head movement.
2
clear: audible laugh, exclamation, raised brows with a forward lean.
3
peak: scream, jump, hands to face, recoiling backwards.

Score every marked moment, including ones you rate 0 or 1 — a low score is a real answer, not a skip.

The cue buttons are optional: if you score ≥ 2, tag whether you judged it from what you saw, what you heard, or both.
